About us


Happy life Christian School is located in Juja Farm, 11km from Juja Town in Kiambu County. The School offers quality education to both the Happy Life children and the surrounding community, from Play group abd Pre-Primary (PP1-PP2) through to Grade 9, covering both Primary and Juniour Secondary lavels, with *** students.

Over the years, we continue to grow - now taking students all the way through Junior Secondary to Grade 9 under the Competency Based Curriculum (CBC)
